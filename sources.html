Verification checklist

  • Find the original document or official record.
  • Confirm the date, institution and reporting period.
  • Check whether the record is complete or updated.
  • Cross-check important claims independently.
  • Keep a note of the exact page, table or paragraph.
  • Correct errors visibly and retain a change log.

Editorial principles

  • Separate fact, allegation, opinion and analysis.
  • Use neutral language and avoid loaded labels.
  • Give subjects fair context and avoid guilt by association.
  • Never publish private personal information.
  • Label demo, estimated and unverified data clearly.
  • Show methodology and limitations alongside metrics.

How a metric should be displayed

Every metric should include its definition, numerator and denominator where relevant, time period, source link, date accessed and any known limitation. If the underlying source is missing, show “Not available” rather than inventing a value.
