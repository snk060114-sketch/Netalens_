Build a fair comparison

  1. Choose a common date range and institution.
  2. Define each indicator before collecting data.
  3. Use official records or clearly explain secondary sources.
  4. Show sample size, missing records and methodology.
  5. Do not turn a descriptive comparison into a claim about motive or character.
