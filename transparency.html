Transparency desk.

A structured way to examine public declarations, declared interests and legal disclosures without jumping to conclusions.

Disclosure is not a verdict

A declaration is a record made under a particular process and date. It does not, by itself, establish wrongdoing. Check the original affidavit or official filing and describe uncertainty precisely.

What to record

  • Document name and issuing authority
  • Filing date and relevant period
  • Exact field or declaration category
  • Original document URL and page number
  • Any limits or missing information

What not to infer

  • Do not infer guilt from an allegation.
  • Do not treat an omission as proof of intent.
  • Do not compare unlike reporting periods.
  • Do not publish private personal information.
