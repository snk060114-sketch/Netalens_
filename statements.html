Statement tracker.

Keep a careful record of public statements. Capture the exact wording, where it appeared, when it was made and what evidence supports the context.

Good research practice

Do not paraphrase a statement as a direct quote. Preserve the original source, date and surrounding context. A tracker entry is not proof that a statement is true or false.
